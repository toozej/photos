## Thoughts

The D300s is still the easiest body in this kit to shoot quickly with confidence. It disappears in use and just gets out of the way.

## Examples

- Event coverage where reliable autofocus matters more than absolute resolution.
- Mixed-light street walks where the older Nikon rendering adds character.
